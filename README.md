# 🛡️ CyberSaathi — Think Before You Click

> **AI-Powered Cyber-Fraud Defense for Bharat 🇮🇳**

CyberSaathi is a cybersecurity platform designed to help everyday users identify and understand potentially fraudulent **SMS messages, emails, job offers, payment scams, phishing attempts, and suspicious URLs** before they become victims.

Instead of simply saying **"This is a scam"**, CyberSaathi explains **why the content may be dangerous**, highlights important red flags, provides a risk assessment, and recommends what the user should do next.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Cybercriminals often don't need to hack a computer first.

They hack **human decision-making**.

Scammers use urgency, fear, rewards, impersonation, fake job offers, KYC warnings, payment requests, and malicious links to manipulate users into taking unsafe actions.

Common examples include:

- 🔐 Fake KYC and account-blocking messages
- 🔗 Phishing URLs
- 💳 UPI and payment scams
- 💼 Fake internship and job offers
- 🎁 Lottery and reward scams
- 🔑 OTP, PIN, password and credential theft
- 😨 Urgency and fear-based social engineering

The challenge is:

> **How can an ordinary user quickly understand whether a digital message or URL is actually dangerous?**

# ⭐ Key Features

### 🔍 Message Analysis
Analyze suspicious SMS, WhatsApp messages, emails, job offers and payment-related messages.

### 🔗 URL Analysis
Evaluate suspicious URLs before interacting with them.

### 🚩 Red Flag Detection
Identify urgency, suspicious links, OTP requests, payment requests, impersonation and other security indicators.

### 📊 Explainable Risk Assessment
Get a risk score, risk level, scam category, detected red flags and recommended actions.

### 🧠 Attack Chain
Visualize how a social-engineering attack can progress from a suspicious message to potential account compromise.

### 🇮🇳 Hindi / Hinglish Guidance
Understand cyber threats through simple, user-friendly explanations.
